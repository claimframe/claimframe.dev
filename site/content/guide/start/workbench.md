Claimframe opens inside one workbench shell. Before a vault is open, the start screen occupies its content area. After you open a vault, the narrow activity bar on the left selects the main workspace. Hover over an icon to see its name; the active workspace is marked in the bar.

## Move between workspaces

The activity bar opens **Capture**, **Entities**, **Query**, **Graph**, **Document Intake**, **Transcript Intake**, **Conflicts**, and **Predicates**. **Vaults** and **Settings** sit at the foot of the bar. **Sources**, **Manage Queries**, and **Manage Vocabularies** are available from **Tools** in the menu bar.

The sidebar changes with the workspace. In **Capture**, choose an entity to insert its name into the capture field. In **Query**, the sidebar shows saved queries and entities; choosing a saved query runs it. **Document Intake**, **Transcript Intake**, and **Conflicts** put their workflow or review queues there. Sidebar sections expand and collapse in a fixed order.

Choose the active activity-bar icon again, or **View → Hide Sidebar**, to give the main task more room. Choose it again or **View → Show Sidebar** to restore the sidebar. Drag the divider to resize it; with the divider focused, use the left and right arrow keys. Double-click the divider to restore its default width.

## Find content and inspect details

The search field in the menu bar finds content in the current vault. Select a result to open the relevant workspace. For a structured assertion search, open **Query** or **Graph** and enter a query there.

Select an assertion to show its provenance and review controls on the right. Use **View → Hide Details** or **Show Details** when an assertion is selected. Graph, transcript, and conflict views also show task-specific details inside their main area.

Use **File** for vault actions and **Tools** for sources, saved queries, predicates, vocabularies, and application settings. **View** also contains **Zoom In**, **Zoom Out**, and **Reset Zoom**. These commands change the interface scale, including the sidebar and main content.
