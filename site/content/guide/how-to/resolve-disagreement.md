1. Open **Conflicts**, or open conflict review from **Claim Review**.
2. Select each participating assertion and compare its source, evidence, capture time, confidence, and exact wording.
3. Use **Edit** only when the stored assertion fails to represent its source correctly.
4. To record a sourced challenge, choose **Dispute** or **Contradict**. Complete the drafted claim with the challenger as source and a reproducible evidence locator.
5. Update each assertion's status to describe its present review state.
6. Confirm that the original claims and the new judgment or challenge remain visible in provenance history.

Do not delete a correctly recorded losing claim merely to clear a conflict indicator. Use `rejected`, `superseded`, or another appropriate status while preserving the original source account.

If a structural conflict appears surprising, review the predicate's cardinality, uniqueness, and conflict settings before judging the claims themselves.
